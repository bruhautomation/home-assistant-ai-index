Notes

Scores microphone audio for the wake word on the ESP32 itself, so no audio leaves the device until after the wake word — the privacy property that distinguishes on-device wake detection from server-side (openWakeWord). This repo is the model framework; ESPHome ships the on-device runtime.

Is this wrong about microWakeWord? That's the bug we care about most — open a correction. Maintainer reports get priority.
