Notes

The cloud inverse of the Whisper add-on: the complete recording of each command leaves your host per request. The "custom" provider accepts any OpenAI-compatible URL, so pointing it at a self-hosted Whisper server keeps audio on your network — that choice, not the integration, decides where your voice goes. Only ever invoked by the pipeline.

Is this wrong about OpenAI Whisper Cloud STT? That's the bug we care about most — open a correction. Maintainer reports get priority.
