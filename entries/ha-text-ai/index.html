Notes

Deliberately reach-less: it cannot see states, history, cameras, or call services. Home data reaches the model only if your automations template it into ask_question calls — which makes it a clean building block when you want AI text without handing over the house. History is stored in its own local .storage.

Is this wrong about HA Text AI? That's the bug we care about most — open a correction. Maintainer reports get priority.
