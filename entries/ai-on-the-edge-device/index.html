Notes

A standalone LAN device rather than HA software — it is its own camera and never touches HA's. It self-runs digitization rounds on a timer and pushes values over MQTT discovery; all TFLite inference happens on the ESP32, so nothing leaves your network unless you add your own upload targets. Trust surface is your MQTT broker and the device's web UI. Community co-maintainers took over from the original author and remain active.

Is this wrong about AI on the Edge Device? That's the bug we care about most — open a correction. Maintainer reports get priority.
