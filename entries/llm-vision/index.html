Notes

Built to be invoked from motion and doorbell automations, so in practice camera frames flow to your chosen provider whenever those triggers fire — pair it with a local backend (Ollama, LocalAI) if that flow shouldn't leave your network. It analyzes and remembers; acting on the result is left to your automations.

Is this wrong about LLM Vision? That's the bug we care about most — open a correction. Maintainer reports get priority.
