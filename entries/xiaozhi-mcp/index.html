Notes

A pure proxy in front of HA's core MCP Server, authenticated with your long-lived token — so everything exposed to Assist becomes reachable from the Xiaozhi cloud over an always-open outbound WebSocket. One finding worth weighing: TLS certificate verification is disabled on that wss:// link in source, so the channel carrying your states trusts any certificate. Quiet since late 2025.

Is this wrong about Xiaozhi MCP? That's the bug we care about most — open a correction. Maintainer reports get priority.
