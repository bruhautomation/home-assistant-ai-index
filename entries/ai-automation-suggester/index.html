Notes

Suggests rather than writes: proposals arrive as notifications and creating the automation stays a human action. It has no built-in schedule — scans run when you call its service, press its button, or fire its update event from your own automations (wire it to a nightly trigger and inventory snapshots flow to your provider unattended, but that wiring is yours).
