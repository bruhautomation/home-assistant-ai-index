What can leave your Home Assistant host:

  • wake-triggered voice audio — to the configured inference server; the firmware default is Tovera's hosted infer.tovera.io
  • transcripts — through the Willow Application Server to your HA Assist pipeline

Notes

The pre-Voice-PE hardware answer, still maintained at a modest pace. The detail that matters: out of the box, audio streams to Tovera's hosted inference server — self-host WIS to keep your voice on your network. Device control is delegated to HA's Assist pipeline via the required Application Server (which also reads /api/states for its entity list); the firmware itself only wakes, records, and streams.

Is this wrong about Willow? That's the bug we care about most — open a correction. Maintainer reports get priority.
