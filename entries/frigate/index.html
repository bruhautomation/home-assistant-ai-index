Notes

Detection (objects, audio events) runs continuously and entirely on your hardware; that is the point of Frigate. The AI-index-relevant switch is GenAI descriptions and semantic search — enabling them sends camera event imagery to the configured provider. Home Assistant connects via the separate frigate-hass-integration.

Is this wrong about Frigate? That's the bug we care about most — open a correction. Maintainer reports get priority.
