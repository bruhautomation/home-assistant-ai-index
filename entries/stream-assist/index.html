Notes

Once its mic switch is on it holds the camera's audio stream open around the clock, running wake-word detection like any satellite — the satellite just happens to be your security camera. Where the audio then goes is decided by the pipeline you select. Dormant: no commits since mid-2024 and a growing issue backlog, though it still works for many. Its only service call is playing TTS on media players.

Is this wrong about Stream Assist? That's the bug we care about most — open a correction. Maintainer reports get priority.
