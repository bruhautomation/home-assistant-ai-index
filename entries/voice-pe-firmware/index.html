Notes

Wake detection runs on the ESP32 itself via microWakeWord, so audio leaves the device only after "Okay Nabu" — and where speech goes after that (local Whisper or a cloud agent) is decided by the pipeline you assign, not by the hardware. The firmware is open and reflashable like any ESPHome device.
