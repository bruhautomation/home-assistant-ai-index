Notes

The browser tab keeps the microphone open and runs openWakeWord/microWakeWord models in-page, only opening an Assist session on detection — so audio stays between the browser and your HA server, and what happens after STT depends on the pipeline you point it at. Needs HTTPS and mic permission. The card also displays camera streams and plays TTS, but that is dashboard display, not AI reach.

Is this wrong about Voice Satellite? That's the bug we care about most — open a correction. Maintainer reports get priority.
