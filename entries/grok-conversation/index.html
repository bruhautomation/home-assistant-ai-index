What it can reach

  • reads statesCan read the live state of entities exposed to it — temperatures, lights, locks, presence.source ↗
  • controls devicesCan call Home Assistant services to switch, dim, unlock, or otherwise act on devices.source ↗

Not granted: reads history · reads camera · microphone · creates automations · edits files · executes code · unattended

Data flow

Providers/backends: xai

What can leave your Home Assistant host:

  • conversation text and exposed entity context — to xAI
  • home_briefing sends a snapshot of up to 80 entity states (locks, alarm, climate) to xAI
  • image services upload local image files, e.g. saved camera snapshots, to xAI
