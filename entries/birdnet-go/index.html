Realtime bird-sound classifier — continuously analyzes microphone or camera (RTSP) audio with local BirdNET models and publishes detections to HA via MQTT discovery.

Notes

The acoustic sibling of Frigate: an always-on pipeline classifying your home's audio locally (TensorFlow Lite), not a voice assistant — filed under perception. It can take audio from RTSP camera streams (video is dropped) as well as microphones. Nothing leaves your network unless you opt into BirdWeather sharing or error telemetry. Very active development; packaged for HA in alexbelgium's add-on repository.

Is this wrong about BirdNET-Go? That's the bug we care about most — open a correction. Maintainer reports get priority.
